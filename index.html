FAQ

Questions traders ask us.

Something else on your mind? Send us a message.

Do I need a smartphone?

MarketBridge works in the browser on any Android phone or iPhone, with no app to install. If you do not have a smartphone, your association can record on your behalf and share your weekly summary.

How much does it cost?

Your first month is free. After that it is GH₵ 30 a month, and members of partner associations pay a lower group rate.

Does it work with mobile money?

Yes. You record MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AT Money payments next to your cash sales, so both add up to what is really in the business.

I have never done accounting. Can I still use it?

Yes. You only enter what you sold, what you spent and how you were paid. MarketBridge does the adding up and explains your week in plain words.

Who can see my records?

Only you, unless you choose to share a summary with your association, a lender or a family member. You can ask for a full copy of your records at any time.

Can my records help me get a loan?

Many lenders ask for proof of steady trading. A few months of weekly summaries gives you clear, printable evidence to take to a bank or savings group. The decision is always the lender’s.
